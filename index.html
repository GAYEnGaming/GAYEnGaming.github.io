How to Install

Follow these steps to install your downloaded pack.

📦 Installation Instructions

  1. Click the Download button for your preferred pack.
  2. Wait for the ZIP file to finish downloading.
  3. Open Minecraft and go to Options.
  4. Open Resource Packs or Shader Packs, depending on the pack.
  5. Open the pack folder and move the downloaded file there.
  6. Return to Minecraft and activate your pack.
